# Example: Investor Pitch (Mission Critical Script)

## User Request
> "Help me create a pitch for Series A investors for my AI scheduling startup"

## Skill Behavior

**Persona detected:** Skeptical Investor
**Framework loaded:** Mission Critical Script (EXCITE → DISTURB → ASSURE)
**Format:** 10-12 slides, 10 minutes verbal
**Tone:** Authoritative + Urgent

## Generated Output Structure

### ACT 1: EXCITE [Slides 1-4, 0-25%]
*What problem are you solving, why are you excited, and why should they care?*

- **Slide 1:** Hook stat — "Teams waste 12 hours/week on scheduling. That's $15,000/employee/year in lost productivity."
- **Slide 2:** Market size — $4.2B scheduling market, growing 23% YoY
- **Slide 3:** The insight — "Calendar AI that understands context, not just availability"
- **Slide 4:** Traction — 2,400 teams, 340% growth last quarter, 94% retention

### ACT 2: DISTURB [Slides 5-8, 25-75%]
*What BIG obstacles stand in the way (and what if we overcame them)?*

- **Slide 5:** The real problem — existing tools only solve 20% of scheduling complexity
- **Slide 6:** What's broken — fragmented calendars, timezone chaos, no priority awareness
- **Slide 7:** Competitive landscape — incumbents are feature-adding, not reimagining
- **Slide 8:** The stakes — first mover in context-aware scheduling captures the category

### ACT 3: ASSURE [Slides 9-12, 75-100%]
*How can we overcome this, why us, and why now?*

- **Slide 9:** The solution — proprietary context engine (demo screenshot)
- **Slide 10:** Why this team — ex-Google Calendar, ex-Calendly, 3 PhDs in NLP
- **Slide 11:** Why now — LLM costs dropped 90% in 18 months; infrastructure moment
- **Slide 12:** The ask — $8M Series A to capture enterprise segment. 18 months to profitability.

---

*This example shows how the Mission Critical framework structures a pitch to excite about the opportunity, disturb with the challenge, then assure with the team and timing.*
