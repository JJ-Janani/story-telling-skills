# Example: 90-Minute Workshop (Save the Cat Beat Sheet)

## User Request
> "Structure a 90-minute workshop on storytelling for my marketing team"

## Skill Behavior

**Persona detected:** Workshop Participants
**Framework loaded:** Save the Cat (15 beats, scaled to 90 min)
**Format:** Slides + activities + handouts
**Tone:** Approachable, encouraging

## Generated Output Structure

### Beat 1: Opening Image [0:00-0:02]
*Show the "before" state*

Activity: Show a terrible internal email that buries the point. Ask: "How many of you have sent something like this?"

### Beat 2: Theme Stated [0:02-0:05]
*State the big idea*

"Every piece of communication is a story. Today you'll learn to tell ones people actually finish reading."

### Beat 3: Setup [0:05-0:09]
*Establish context*

Quick audit: participants rate their last 3 emails/decks on a 1-5 "would I read this?" scale.

### Beat 4: Catalyst [0:09-0:11]
*The triggering event*

Reveal: "Research shows 80% of business communications are never read to completion. Yours might be in that pile."

### Beat 5: Debate [0:11-0:22]
*Wrestling with the challenge*

Group exercise: Teams diagnose WHY their communications fail. Categories: no hook, no structure, no payoff, wrong audience.

### Beat 6: Break into Act 2 [0:22-0:24]
*Commitment to learn*

"Here's the good news: there are only 10 frameworks, and you only need 2-3 for 90% of your work."

### Beat 7: B-Story [0:24-0:27]
*The human angle*

Personal story from facilitator about a presentation that failed — and the one framework that fixed it.

### Beat 8: Fun & Games [0:27-0:45]
*Core skill building — the promise of the workshop delivered*

Teach 3 frameworks (Minto, StoryBrand, Mission Critical) with live examples. Pairs practice: rewrite their audit email using one framework.

### Beat 9: Midpoint [0:45-0:48] ☕ BREAK
*The "aha" moment*

Pairs share rewrites. Facilitator highlights the best transformations. Group sees the before/after contrast.

### Beat 10: Bad Guys Close In [0:48-0:60]
*Complications and challenges*

Harder exercise: "What about when you have multiple audiences? Conflicting goals? No time?" Introduce combinations and personas.

### Beat 11: All is Lost [1:00-1:03]
*The hardest truth*

"The #1 reason storytelling fails in business isn't skill — it's skipping the audience question. You write for yourself."

### Beat 12: Dark Night of the Soul [1:03-1:06]
*Reflection*

Solo journaling: "Think about your most important upcoming communication. Who is it REALLY for? What do THEY need?"

### Beat 13: Break into Act 3 [1:06-1:08]
*The key insight*

"The framework isn't the magic. The AUDIENCE QUESTION is the magic. The framework just makes the answer actionable."

### Beat 14: Finale [1:08-1:25]
*Capstone exercise*

Each person outlines their real upcoming communication using the full skill: audience → framework → adaptation. Peer feedback in trios.

### Beat 15: Final Image [1:25-1:30]
*Mirror the opening — show transformation*

Revisit the terrible email from Beat 1. As a group, rewrite it live using everything learned. Compare before/after on screen. Applause.

*This example shows how Save the Cat's 15 beats pace a 90-minute workshop with modality switches, energy management, and a satisfying transformation arc.*
